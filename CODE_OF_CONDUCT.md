# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about ideas and generous with people.
- Critique sources, claims, and code, not contributors.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a factual claim matters.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information without consent.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, including commercial relationships.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and unwanted sexual attention are not tolerated. Spam, undisclosed affiliate links, and deliberately misleading submissions are also violations.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Report concerns privately to the maintainer, [@Saeed-gondle](https://github.com/Saeed-gondle), through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
